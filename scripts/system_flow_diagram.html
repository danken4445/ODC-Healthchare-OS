Public Healthcare & No Balance Billing (NBB) Workflow

Statutory ₱0 copay intake, nurse triage, clinical documentation, Statement of Account (SOA), PhilHealth eClaims dossier, and inter-facility specialist referral.

RA 11223 Universal Health Care Act
PhilHealth Circular 2017-0017 (NBB)
₱0

Dual-Track Accounting Under No Balance Billing

Under statutory NBB rules, patients are never billed (₱0.00 patient liability). However, standard catalog chargemaster prices must still be recorded on every line item in the subledger (financial_ledger_entries) to substantiate PhilHealth Case Rate 1 / Case Rate 2 reimbursement claims and COA audit compliance.

STAGE 01

Public Intake & NBB Waiver

Facility set to is_government_no_billing. Pay-in-full rule waived; instant booked confirmation with ₱0 invoice.

STAGE 02

Arrival & Nurse Triage

Lobby QR check-in, per-doctor queue sequence (e.g. RHU-001), baseline vitals, and DOH-PIDSR pre-screen.

STAGE 03

Doctor Encounter & PMR

Visual body mapping, structured SOAP notes, ICD-10 coding, and Philippine National Formulary (PNF) e-prescriptions.

STAGE 04

NBB SOA & eClaims Dossier

FEFO pharmacy stock relief, Statement of Account with ₱0 patient due, and automated assembly of CF1, CF2, and CF4 dossiers.

STAGE 05

Inter-Facility Referral

Digital PMR referral packet with cryptographic verification QR, continuous NBB portability, and zero duplicate testing.
